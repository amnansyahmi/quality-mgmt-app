WORKSPACE GUIDE

Roles & workflow help

Requestor

Raise and correct complaints

Create a complaint, save Part A as a draft, add immediate actions and upload supporting evidence. Submit to the QM evaluation queue. If returned, correct and resubmit the same reference.

You can review your submitted complaints. Investigation and approval belong to the assigned roles.

Owner

Investigate and carry out actions

For assigned complaints, complete Part B evaluation, root cause and action plan. Save reporting answers for QM review. Submit the plan for HOD then QM approval. Complete the approved actions and add dates and evidence. Request a delay or extension from HOD when needed.

You can correct returned work. Approved action-plan descriptions, owners and dates stay locked during completion.

HOD

Review department work

Review assigned action plans and completed actions. Approve and forward to a named QM, or return to the owner with a reason. Decide response-delay and extension requests.

You can only decide tasks assigned to you. Required QM review cannot be skipped.

QM

Evaluate, verify and review closure

Evaluate submitted complaints and assign the department, Owner, HOD, QM and CM. Confirm reportability after the owner saves the evaluation. Review action plans and completion after HOD, record effectiveness checks, and review closure before CM.

Only the assigned QM makes record-specific decisions. Unassigned evaluation tasks are shared by the active QM queue.

CM

Approve final closure

Review the assigned closure package, effectiveness evidence and QM review. Approve and close, or return to QM review or effectiveness correction with a reason.

Final closure follows QM review. Closed records are read only.

Admin · your current role

Manage the workspace

Create and maintain people, active accounts and roles. Import source forms in Form Studio, review fields, submitter permissions, workflow stages and return destinations, then publish a module.

Administrator access lets you inspect records; it does not bypass task ownership or sign approvals for another role.

Complaint sequence

  1. Requestor · notification and evidence
  2. QM · evaluation and assignment
  3. Owner + QM · investigation, action plan and reporting review
  4. HOD → QM · approve plan
  5. Owner · complete approved actions
  6. HOD → QM · review completed actions
  7. QM → CM · effectiveness, closure review and final closure

Returning keeps the reference and history. The recipient corrects the work and repeats the required reviews.

Where to go

My task inbox is the place to act on assigned complaints and Form Library records. Complaint register is for searching and reviewing complaints; its Due & overdue view highlights deadlines. Drafts & resubmissions contains the Requestor’s unfinished notifications. Overview shows complaint analytics.

Form Library contains published modules. A module’s configured submitter roles and named task holders control its workflow; these can differ from the complaint process above.

Notifications

In-app notifications arrive when a task is passed or returned to you, an exception needs a decision, or a record is completed. Open a notification to mark it read and review the record. Marking it read does not complete the task. Older notifications may point to a task that has already moved on.

The bell refreshes every 30 seconds while this workspace is open. Complaint email delivery requires a configured SMTP relay; Form Library notifications are currently in-app.
